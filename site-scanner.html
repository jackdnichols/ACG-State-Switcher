Site Scanner

Scanners you can point at any site: lower-environment link leaks, broken links, missing images, mixed content, spelling/typos, a basic SEO/accessibility page audit, free-text word search, and live console error capture with fix recommendations.

Works on any http(s) site. Each scan stays on the origin of its own Start URL.
Runs as its own extension tab, not injected into the page, so requests to the target site are cross-site fetches. Cookies marked SameSite=Lax/Strict on the target site (the default for most session cookies) will not be sent, so scans see the same content a logged-out visitor would even if you're signed in on another tab.

Mixed content scanner log
A scan, not a live monitor: drives one of your open tabs through same-origin pages one at a time (reload, then watch for a fixed window), catching uncaught exceptions, unhandled promise rejections, and console.error/console.warn calls — including page-init/tag-manager errors, since the hook is in place before each page's own scripts run. Your browser tab will visibly navigate through the site while this runs; don't use it for anything else until the scan finishes or you click Stop. It does not catch browser-native "failed to load resource" network log lines (use Broken Links/Missing Images for those) or anything logged inside a cross-origin iframe (chat widgets, ad tags, etc. — those have their own isolated console). The same message repeating back-to-back on one page collapses into one entry with a repeat count.
The tab the scan will drive. Defaults to the tab this scanner was opened from, if any.
